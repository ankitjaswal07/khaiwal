About us

{{site}} is an independent information website that keeps a clear, dated record of published game results. Each game has its own page with a monthly table, a short quick answer and plain explanations of how to read the record. A full chart page puts every game side by side, and a notice bar on every page carries the latest published numbers.

We exist to make a simple job easier: finding a published number for a particular game and date without hunting through cluttered pages. Results are entered by our editors from the Publish results screen, shown in India Standard Time, and left blank until they are published. We never predict, guess or suggest numbers.

We do not take bets, entries, deposits or payments of any kind, and nothing on this website invites anyone to play. If you have a correction, a question or a suggestion, please use the contact page and we will get back to you.

FAQs

What is this website?

{{site}} is an information website. It records published game results by date, in India Standard Time, and presents them in tables and charts that are easy to read on a phone or a computer.

Do you predict or suggest numbers?

No. We never predict, guess or recommend numbers, and nothing here should be read as a tip. Past results do not say anything reliable about future ones.

Can I play, bet or pay on this website?

No. This website does not accept bets, entries or payments and has no link to any service that does. It is a reference site only.

How are results updated?

Our editors enter each result from the Publish results screen once it has been announced. Until then, the game shows a bouncing WAIT word instead of a number.

What does WAIT mean?

WAIT means that today’s result for that game has not been published yet. It is a status, not a number, and it is replaced automatically when the result goes live.

What does a dash mean in a table?

A dash means no result has been published for that date. It does not mean zero and it is never filled in with an estimate.

Which time zone do you use?

Every date and time on this website is India Standard Time (IST). Convert it if you are reading from another country.

How do I report a mistake or contact you?

Use the contact page. Please mention the game and date so that we can check it quickly.

Can software or AI assistants use your data?

Yes. Each game page links to a JSON version of the month it shows, and the site publishes a machine-readable description at /llms.txt and an open API description at /wp-json/khaiwal/v1/openapi.

Privacy policy

This policy explains what information {{site}} handles when you visit the website. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Contact form: if you write to us we store the name, email address, optional phone number and message you send, so that we can reply. This information is visible only to the site administrators and editors.
  • Language choice: if you switch between English and Hindi, your choice is remembered in your own browser. It is not sent to us.
  • Spam protection: to stop automated form abuse we keep a one-way scrambled marker of your connection for about a minute. We do not store your IP address in the contact list.
  • Logged-in users: editors and administrators receive the standard WordPress sign-in cookies. Visitors do not need an account.

What we do not do

We do not sell or rent personal information, we do not run advertising networks on this website, and the fonts and images are served from this site rather than from third-party trackers. If a plugin added by the site owner collects more data, such as analytics or SEO tools, it is the owner’s responsibility to say so here.

How long we keep contact messages

Messages are kept for as long as they are useful for answering you and for our own records, and can be deleted by an administrator at any time. To ask us to delete or correct a message you sent, use the contact page.
